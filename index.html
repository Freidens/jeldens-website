Who are we?

Jeldens Products is a food products manufacturer that focuses on spicy products such as Chili Garlic Oil, Spicy Suka, Alamang, and GInamos. As a former Message Spa, we also sell our best selling Jeldens Herbal Oil.

The products are sold mainly through our partnered distributors around Pantukan, Davao de Oro and Tagum City, Davao del Norte. If you are interested in partnering with us, kindly contact us by clicking the contact us button below.
